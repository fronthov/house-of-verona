# House of Verona — proposed franchise control framework (legal drafting notes)
Status: internal proposal for professional legal review, NOT a franchise offer, executed agreement or guarantee against future disputes.

## Preliminary terms shared publicly
- Territory and premises: proposed **minimum carpet/usable floor area 800 sq ft**. Verify permitted premises class, minimum frontage, seating, fire exits, access, storage, food safety and local approvals during site review.
- Initial term: proposed five years. Define commencement date, opening obligations, cure periods, renewal and termination procedures in the executed document.
- Ongoing franchise fee: **2% annual royalty calculated on "Total Sales"**, with the exact formula negotiated and recorded before signature. No alternative 5% brand-support fee applies.
- Commercial investment/initial franchise pricing is confidential and **not to be placed in a public repository, form, page, SEO metadata or marketing document**. Share the proposed price privately only if the applicant specifically requests it. Confirm taxation and applicable fees in the term sheet.
- No franchise is promised by completing an enquiry form. No refundable or nonrefundable deposit should be solicited without a documented written instrument.

## Safeguards to develop with qualified commercial counsel
1. **Total Sales definition.** Base includes agreed applicable business sales channels (dine-in, takeaway, online marketplaces, catering, merchandise and authorised outlets), with transparent treatment of billed GST, legitimate refunds, returns, cancellations, discounts and reimbursements. Define whether the royalty base uses GST-exclusive net sales or gross receivables; do not leave "total sales" unqualified.
2. **Reporting and audit.** Link approved POS and invoicing systems; monthly provisional reports, quarterly reconciliation, annual royalty statement, independent audit rights with reasonable notice, document preservation, error correction and late-payment cure. Do not demand access to unrelated personal finances.
3. **Brand and intellectual property.** Licensed usage limited to approved marks, recipes, training materials, equipment designs and trade dress; quality control, permitted advertising, brand guidelines and termination of use after valid exit. Confirm the registered trademark owner and its authority to license the House of Verona marks.
4. **Territory and expansion.** Explicit map-based territory if exclusivity offered; define its limits, delivery radius, cross-border/online sales handling and circumstances in which new outlets may operate nearby. Never imply exclusivity in an enquiry.
5. **Operations and food compliance.** FSSAI and local authority licences, food safety, labelling, hygiene SOPs, employment obligations, workplace safety, waste management, recordkeeping, recall procedures, supplier eligibility and traceable origin claims. Respect local laws.
6. **Investment and support.** Exactly define set-up obligations, estimated capital costs, items included, training hours, equipment and approved suppliers, maintenance, opening criteria, marketing contributions and timelines; no promise of revenue.
7. **Confidentiality and data protection.** Confidential information exclusions; proportionate access and deletion/retention; restrictions on customer and applicant data, payment processor roles and incident response.
8. **Risk allocation and consumer remedies.** Insurance limits, third-party injury/customer claims, reasonable indemnities, compliance audits, operational corrective steps and consumer grievance responsibilities without excluding mandatory legal remedies.
9. **Default, cure, renewal and exit.** Specific defaults, written notice, time to cure, dispute escalation and appropriate termination/renewal fees disclosed in advance; inventory and unredeemed customer obligation reconciliation; post-termination brand usage limits. Avoid overbroad non-competes or unreasonably punitive clauses.
10. **Dispute resolution.** Indian governing law and properly drafted jurisdiction/arbitration clauses as appropriate, with notice mechanism and emergency relief that does not improperly override mandatory consumer or statutory remedies.

No commercial arrangement is "loophole-free". Protecting House of Verona involves clearly scoped terms, fair disclosure, enforceability, careful recordkeeping and a qualified Indian franchise/commercial lawyer.
