Customer Support & Grievances

Classy service begins with listening—and responding.

Prepared 16 November 2026 · Applicable to website order requests for India

Contact House of Verona

For general information, product questions, order changes, delivery concerns and complaints, contact WhatsApp House of Verona or info@houseofverona.in. Include your order reference whenever available. Website order requests and payment confirmations are handled manually.

What to include

Provide your order ID, the product or service concerned, what happened and how you would like us to help. Photos of damaged packaging or a product label may help us investigate a quality or courier issue. Never send a login code or password.

Complaint handling

We aim to acknowledge consumer grievances within 48 hours and work to resolve them within one month, consistent with applicable e-commerce requirements. If additional investigation is needed, we will communicate with you about progress and the available remedy.

Franchise & partnership enquiries

For franchise, partnership and business enquiries, use the same official contact details: info@houseofverona.in or WhatsApp +91 8799817395. Our team will review enquiries and respond directly. No franchise availability or approval is implied by sending an enquiry.
