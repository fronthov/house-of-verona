Order Cancellation

Your order should move forward only after the details are clear.

Prepared 16 November 2026 · Applicable to website order requests for India

Before approval or payment

A website checkout creates an order request. It is not an automatic payment or guaranteed stock reservation. If you wish to withdraw a request before payment, message us with its order reference; no website payment exists to reverse at this stage.

After payment, before dispatch

Please request cancellation as soon as possible through WhatsApp or our contact email, quoting the order ID. We will check whether the parcel has been prepared or handed to the courier and confirm what can be cancelled and what refund, if any, is due.

We do not impose undisclosed automatic cancellation fees. Any permitted charges would be explained before acceptance and must comply with applicable law.

After dispatch or delivery

If an order is already with the courier, contact us before refusing or returning a parcel so that we can discuss the available process and any applicable return transport costs. Defective, incorrect, damaged or otherwise non-conforming goods remain subject to the Returns & Refunds Policy and applicable consumer protections.

If House of Verona cannot fulfil

If stock, serviceability or another issue prevents us from fulfilling an approved order, we will contact you about a suitable alternative, revised timing or cancellation. Any consideration paid for an order we cancel will be refunded as legally required.

Contact

For assistance, contact WhatsApp House of Verona or orders@houseofverona.in with the order ID.
