GROW WITH THE HOUSE · FRANCHISE PARTNERSHIPS

Build the experience. Protect the standard.

We welcome hospitality-minded entrepreneurs who share our belief in exceptional Indian-origin tea and coffee, thoughtfully prepared food and sincere service.

A House of Verona partnership begins with compatibility, not with a cheque. We review each proposed location, the operator's hospitality experience, available space, financial readiness and the local market to determine whether the House can deliver a consistent guest experience.

800 sq ftMinimum proposed outlet area
5 yearsInitial proposed contract term
2%Yearly royalty on total sales
PrivateInvestment terms shared on specific request

The royalty is proposed at 2% of total sales for the relevant financial year. The precise definition of sales, GST treatment, refunds, reporting, audit access and payment due dates must be set out transparently in the definitive franchise agreement.

We do not display commercial investment or initial franchise pricing publicly. If you specifically request those details, our team can share the applicable commercial information privately after reviewing your enquiry.

How applications are reviewed

Submit an expression of interest with your proposed city and state, site area in square feet, location type, investment capacity and relevant experience. The enquiry is recorded securely and routed to the House of Verona team at info@houseofverona.in. Applications are not binding offers and do not create franchise or territorial rights.

Next, qualified applicants may be invited to discuss the commercial proposal, operational standards, training, supply arrangements, technology, brand guidelines, compliance and a draft agreement. Any outlet proposal remains subject to site inspection, due diligence and written approval.

Quality cannot be franchised by a logo alone.

Our operating model is intended to protect bean quality, recipe consistency, food safety, clear pricing and caring hospitality. An eventual agreement should address permitted trademarks, sourcing standards, marketing permissions, inspections, sales reporting, confidentiality, renewals, exits and fair mechanisms for resolving disputes. No agreement can eliminate every legal or commercial risk.

Apply for a franchise

No application fee. No guaranteed income, territorial exclusivity, franchise approval or investment return. Final terms and statutory disclosures require professional legal review.
