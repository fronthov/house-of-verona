Shipping & Delivery

Precise preparation. Clear dispatch expectations. Every order reviewed before payment.

Prepared 16 November 2026 · Applicable to website order requests for India

Where we deliver

We currently accept requests for delivery within India, subject to courier serviceability at the delivery PIN code. International orders are not yet available. We confirm coverage and any destination-specific limitations before asking for payment.

Dispatch: 3–5 working days

Confirmed, paid orders are dispatched within 3–5 working days after payment verification. This describes the time before the parcel leaves House of Verona; it is not a guaranteed delivery date.

Courier transit times depend on the destination, courier and local conditions. We will share the available dispatch and delivery information with you as your order is confirmed. If we cannot meet a promised dispatch or delivery schedule, we will contact you to discuss your options.

Shipping charges

For tea and coffee orders, standard shipping is ₹150. Shipping is free only when the eligible tea-and-coffee amount after applicable Veronates discounts is strictly above ₹1,999. At ₹1,999 or below, the standard charge applies. The quote shows the item total, membership savings, shipping and order total before approval.

Eligible tea and coffee must total at least 250 g per basket. The founder's book is currently enquiry-only and is not covered by this tea-and-coffee shipping offer.

How the order works

Adding items and submitting the website form creates an order request, not a completed purchase. The request is saved and a WhatsApp message is prepared. Our team confirms stock, destination and final amount before sharing manual payment instructions. No payment is collected automatically through the website.

After your order is dispatched, request courier/tracking details from our team if they have not yet been provided. Keep your order reference for all enquiries.

Need help?

If there is a delivery concern, contact us with your order reference through WhatsApp House of Verona or orders@houseofverona.in. Our Returns & Refunds Policy explains what happens if your parcel is damaged, incorrect, defective or significantly late.
