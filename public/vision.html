THE ORIGIN OF OUR PHILOSOPHY

We invested in the cup before the room.

A story of Indian origins, thoughtful hospitality and a different definition of luxury.

There is a question behind House of Verona: when someone remembers a café, do they remember the weight of its interiors—or how the coffee tasted, how the food was made, and how welcome they felt?

We do not dismiss beautiful spaces. Atmosphere matters. But we refused to make expensive décor the measure of quality. Our first commitment is to what is served: exceptional beans, expressive tea, thoughtful food, careful preparation and service that is genuinely warm. The surroundings should support that experience, never distract from an ordinary cup.

India's story, from the mist to the mountains.

Across India, geography quietly shapes flavour. In the Northeast, mist, altitude, soil and seasons influence the character of tea. Farther south, the coffee landscapes of Karnataka, Kerala, Tamil Nadu and Andhra Pradesh offer their own distinctive possibilities through variety, processing and craft.

These are not just place names to print on a menu. They are invitations to ask where a cup began, how it was grown and processed, and what care can reveal at the table. We seek to work with verifiable origins and explain them without confusing jargon or exaggerated claims. One House. An entire country of exceptional beans and leaves.

Premium should not mean distant.

House of Verona is a premium all-day café—not simply a speciality coffee counter. Coffee and tea are central to our identity, but so are vegetarian food, human connection, comfort and the freedom to spend time well. Our aim is for morning office-goers, curious students, creators, families and friends to experience quality on equal terms.

We take inspiration from the quiet precision of Japanese speciality service: clean presentation, deliberate brewing, considered ceramics and small gestures of care. Yet the warmth is distinctly ours. A guest should never need to know technical vocabulary to deserve an exceptional cup.

Fairness in every cup.

Fairness begins with clarity: what we are serving, why an origin matters, how we prepare it and what a guest is paying for. We believe value can be premium without becoming intimidating. It also means listening when a cup is not right, treating people respectfully and taking accountability for the promises we make.

We would rather spend our energy finding and brewing expressive beans than constructing the appearance of quality through unnecessary spectacle. The difference should be something you can taste.

The world can wait.

Life moves quickly. A good café offers a small exception: a moment to notice aroma, share a meal, learn something about a bean, or simply feel that no one is rushing you away. That is the House we want to build, in Nagpur and wherever our standards can be carried responsibly.

India in Every Sip. Fairness in Every Cup. Luxury, Normalised. THE WORLD CAN WAIT.

Dishant RD Founder, House of Verona · Author and coffee educator
