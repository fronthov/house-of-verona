Privacy Policy

Your information should receive the same care as your order.

Prepared 16 November 2026 · Applicable to website order requests for India

Who operates this service

House of Verona operates the website at houseofverona.in. Customer support is available by WhatsApp at +91 8799817395. Information we collect

When you use the store, we may process your account email and one-time-code login requests; name, telephone number, delivery address and PIN code; product choices, order amounts, order history and order-status records; and membership status; franchise applicant details including proposed location, area, experience and indicative investment capacity; and membership payment reference and verification records.

For site functionality and security, hosting systems may receive technical information such as IP address, request timestamps and browser information. Your shopping bag may be stored in your browser's local storage.

How we use information

We use franchise application data only to evaluate and respond to business enquiries, and membership payment references to verify payment and activate eligibility. We use relevant data to authenticate account access, calculate quotes, process and verify order requests, coordinate WhatsApp checkout and manual payments, fulfil shipments, provide support, keep transaction records and protect the service against abuse.

We do not use order contact details for unrelated promotional messaging without an appropriate basis or permission.

Service providers and messages

The site uses Cloudflare for hosting and data storage, Resend to deliver sign-in codes and WhatsApp if you choose to send an order message. Courier or other fulfilment providers may receive the minimum necessary delivery information after an order is approved. Those providers handle information under their own terms where applicable.

Payment is currently coordinated manually after founder confirmation. Tea and coffee order payments are coordinated manually. If membership checkout is enabled, Razorpay handles payment collection; House of Verona does not store full card details. Membership records retain payment references for verification and dispute handling. Do not share passwords or one-time login codes over WhatsApp.

Cookies and local storage

The website uses a session cookie to keep signed-in customers authenticated and browser local storage for the shopping bag. These are functional storage mechanisms. A customer may clear local storage through their browser, though this may remove the saved bag.

Retention and your choices

We keep personal data as needed for the stated purposes and applicable records, dispute, safety and legal requirements. Specific retention schedules are being documented as part of operational launch preparation.

To request access, correction, withdrawal of a consent where applicable, or deletion of eligible information, contact us using the details below. We will review requests and any applicable obligations before taking action.

Questions or complaints

Reach us at WhatsApp House of Verona or info@houseofverona.in. More details are available under Customer Support & Grievances. We may update this notice when the website or our procedures change.
