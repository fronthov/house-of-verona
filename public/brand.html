The Seal

Our full House of Verona seal combines the name of the House with a crowned-woman profile, flowing hair and a star-filled spiral. It identifies House of Verona, a Nagpur-based premium all-day café for Indian specialty coffee, orthodox tea, vegetarian accompaniments and thoughtful hospitality.

The Emblem

The gold-on-emerald emblem is our distinctive visual mark across coffee cups, tea service and branded materials. Our coffee and tea story begins with India's growing regions and estates, and with a belief that an excellent cup should be approachable.
