Website Terms

A considered collection, ordered through a transparent approval process.

Prepared 16 November 2026 · Applicable to website order requests for India

Using this website

This website presents House of Verona products, current indicative availability and a way to send order requests. Prices are shown in Indian rupees. Customers should provide accurate contact and delivery details and must not misuse account or checkout functions.

Product details and availability

Coffee and tea descriptions, origin information, pack weights and prices are shown in the product listing. Specific estates, harvests and stock availability are not guaranteed unless verified and confirmed with you. Product images may be illustrative where stated.

The founder's coffee book is currently displayed for enquiry only and is not available for direct website checkout.

Requests, confirmation and payments

Placing an order through the website records a request and prepares a WhatsApp message. It does not automatically charge you. House of Verona reviews the request, stock, destination and totals. A purchase proceeds only when the final details are confirmed and payment arrangements are agreed.

Never treat a message from an unverified third party as official payment instructions. Confirm any payment request through our published contact details.

Prices, minimum order and Veronates

The minimum eligible tea-and-coffee basket weight is 250 g. Eligible verified Veronates members receive quantity discounts across combined eligible tea and coffee weight: 5% at 500 g, 7% at 1 kg, 15% only above 2 kg and 25% only above 5 kg. The book does not receive the weight discount. Membership eligibility is verified by the store.

Standard shipping is ₹150 for eligible tea and coffee orders, waived only when the eligible post-discount subtotal is above ₹1,999. The review screen calculates the actual order amount before approval.

Veronates one-year membership

A purchased Veronates membership is a one-year, non-transferable account benefit. Its validity begins on the server-recorded confirmation of a successfully captured Razorpay payment and ends at the corresponding calendar anniversary, at the same time in India Standard Time. A purchase on 29 February expires on 28 February the following year. Discounts cease when the expiry time is reached, without automatic renewal.

Promotional membership is listed at ₹999 for purchases through 27 October 2027 (IST), then ₹9,999 beginning 28 October 2027. This is a price per one-year membership, not a lifetime benefit. Any later renewal requires a separate new purchase at the then-published price and terms. Existing members with separately agreed legacy entitlements are subject to their own documented agreement.

After verified payment the House generates a personal membership card with the holder's name, unique ID, activation time and expiry time. We email it to the sign-in address; the member can also sign in to view or print it. The member ID itself does not authorise discounts without account verification.

Eligible tea and coffee quantity discounts are currently 5% from 500 g, 7% from 1 kg, 15% only above 2 kg and 25% only above 5 kg, calculated on the eligible order weight. Food and books are excluded.

Automatic membership activation requires verification of the captured Razorpay payment by our server. No automatic renewal or recurring debit is scheduled. Statutory consumer cancellation/refund rights continue to apply. If a payment was captured but membership activation or email delivery fails, contact info@houseofverona.in with the payment ID; do not submit a second payment before the first is reconciled.
