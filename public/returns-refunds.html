Returns & Refunds

Fairness in every cup means we listen when something is not right.

Prepared 16 November 2026 · Applicable to website order requests for India

When to contact us

Please contact us if a product is damaged in transit, defective, missing, incorrect, not as described, or does not meet an agreed delivery commitment. Provide the order reference, a brief explanation and, where possible, photos of the package, labels and product.

Reporting damage promptly—ideally within 48 hours of delivery—helps preserve courier evidence. This request is not a waiver of any statutory consumer rights.

Food products and change of mind

Tea and coffee are consumable products. We generally cannot resell opened or used food packs. A change-of-mind return of a correctly supplied, safe product is considered individually rather than automatically accepted. Please contact us before sending any parcel back.

This does not limit remedies that may be required by law for defective, deficient, spurious, misdescribed or unduly late products.

Review and resolution

We will review the order record and available evidence and discuss an appropriate resolution, such as correction, replacement, return or refund as applicable. If a return is required, we will give you return instructions before you ship anything.

For a verified House of Verona error or qualifying defective item, return transport arrangements and any associated cost will be confirmed by us. For any discretionary change-of-mind return we agree to accept, we will disclose return-shipping charges, if any, in advance.

Refunds

No payment is collected at online order-request submission. For an order that has already been paid, approved refunds will be processed using an agreed legitimate payment method after verification. We will confirm the amount, method and expected processing time when the resolution is approved; bank processing times may vary.

We will not refuse a legally required refund merely because a food pack has been opened to discover a defect or other covered problem.

Request assistance

Contact WhatsApp House of Verona or orders@houseofverona.in and include your order ID. You can also review our Cancellation Policy and Customer Support details.
