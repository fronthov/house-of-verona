FOUNDER'S VISION · DISHANT RD

Luxury, normalised. India in every sip.

We believe luxury is not what surrounds you; it is what you experience. House of Verona was imagined as a place where Indian-origin coffee and tea, thoughtful food, precise brewing and generous hospitality belong to everyone who values quality.

From the tea-growing landscapes of India's Northeast to the coffee estates of the South, our purpose is to celebrate origins without unnecessary pretence. We respect the bean, care for the guest and seek fairness in each cup.

India in Every Sip. Fairness in Every Cup. The World Can Wait.
