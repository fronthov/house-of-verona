THE FOUNDER'S VISION · DISHANT RD

We chose exceptional beans over extravagant interiors.

We could have built a café around an elaborate room and an ordinary cup. Instead, we chose a more demanding beginning: to invest our attention where it truly matters—in Indian-origin beans and leaves, their freshness, the brew, the food on your table and how you are treated.

From the mist-covered tea gardens of the Northeast to the coffee-growing landscapes of South India, there is extraordinary character to discover close to home. House of Verona exists to bring that journey closer: a premium all-day café where quality, honest value and genuinely caring service belong together.

Luxury should not demand distance or pretence. It should feel natural, informed and welcoming—whether you are here for a quick espresso, a comforting tea, a thoughtful meal, a conversation or a long pause.
