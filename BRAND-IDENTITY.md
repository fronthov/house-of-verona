# HOUSE OF VERONA — FIXED BRAND IDENTITY

## Official identity — do not rename
- **Brand name:** HOUSE OF VERONA
- **One official tagline:** **INDIA IN EVERY SIP**
- Keep that exact wording, spacing and uppercase treatment on every website header, footer, brand lockup, package mockup, social graphic, printed menu and future SEO page.
- Never create an alternate tagline as part of search optimisation. The tagline is brand identity, not a ranking keyword.

## Brand-supporting language — not taglines
- Luxury is what you consume, not how heavy or expensive the interiors look.
- Respect your hard-earned money; spend on quality.
- An evolving, origin-led selection of Indian estate-grown coffees and orthodox teas in one House.
- Transparent sourcing claims, vegetarian food, thoughtful service, takeaway and tailored group enquiries.
- Phrases such as 'Fairness in Every Cup', 'Luxury, Normalised', or 'The World Can Wait' may be used sparingly as non-logo editorial copy only if founder-approved, not displayed next to the logo as alternate taglines.

## Search engine copy
- Titles and descriptions can target 'specialty coffee in Nagpur', 'orthodox tea Nagpur', 'premium cafe in Nagpur', 'takeaway', 'gym packs', and 'office packages'.
- Keep the brand name and tagline unchanged even when SEO titles change.
- Do not claim that all Indian estates are stocked at all times or claim exclusivity without evidence.
- Do not invent prices, health outcomes, provenance or supply commitments.
